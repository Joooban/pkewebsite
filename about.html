Our story

Built in Davao, run for working kitchens

Premium Kitchen Equipment (PKE) supplies and services commercial kitchen equipment out of Davao City — refrigeration, cooking lines, food prep, smallwares, and catering gear for restaurants, hotels, and catering operations across Mindanao. We're not a general appliance store: everything we carry is rated for continuous commercial service, and we back it with parts, technicians, and people who pick up the phone.

Whether you're fitting out a new kitchen from scratch or replacing one unit that's about to cost you a service, our team can spec the right equipment for your volume and your space.
